# StackTask — "Break Through" cinematic brief

**Deliverable:** one 3–5 second clip, 16:9, 1920×1080, 24fps, MP4 (H.264) + optional
WebM/ProRes with alpha. No audio needed. No text, no logos, no watermark.

**Subject:** three cube characters from the StackTask logo — **one coral-orange
(#e58a67) and two blue (#4b89f7)**, isometric dice-like boxes with **thick black
outlines**, flat fills, no gradients on the cubes themselves. They are the heroes.
They have no faces, no limbs — personality comes purely from how they tumble,
squash and land.

---

## STYLE SPEC (repeat this to any tool or animator)

Flat 2D anime/comic style, cel-shaded, clean confident linework, **moderate detail
— readable and graphic, not hyper-rendered**. Colors natural and slightly muted,
**not oversaturated, not washed out** — think a modern sports anime TV production,
not a glossy 3D render and not a flat vector infographic. Two-to-three tone shading
per surface, hard-edged shadows. Comic-style speed lines, motion blur streaks and
impact bursts. Sunny daylight, warm directional sun. Camera has weight — slight
handheld shake on impact. Absolutely no photorealism, no 3D CGI look, no text.

---

## SEQUENCE — 4 shots, ~4 seconds total

### SHOT 1 — SIDE TRACKING (0.0–1.2s)
Locked **side profile** view, camera tracking laterally at speed. Three cubes
**roll and tumble down a winding mountain dirt path**, kicking up dust and small
stones. Green mountain slopes, pine trees and distant peaks whip past in the
background at high parallax speed. Horizontal comic speed lines streak across the
frame. The coral cube leads; the two blue cubes tumble just behind and slightly
staggered. They **bounce off the ground** with real weight, squashing slightly on
each impact and stretching in the air.

### SHOT 2 — OVERTAKING RUNNERS (1.2–2.2s)
Same side-on tracking angle, pulled back slightly. **Human runners in athletic
gear** are sprinting down the same mountain path. The three cubes come **barrelling
in from behind and overtake them**, rolling past fast and low. Runners react —
heads turning, hair and jerseys blown back in the wash. Dust trails and speed lines
intensify. Cubes stay sharply in focus while runners blur with motion.

### SHOT 3 — FIRST-PERSON CUBE POV (2.2–3.0s)
Hard cut to **first-person point of view from inside the lead cube**. The camera
**rolls and tumbles with the cube** — the horizon spins over and over as the world
somersaults. Ground rushes up, sky sweeps past, ground again — fast, disorienting,
exhilarating. Heavy radial motion blur at the frame edges, white speed lines
converging toward the center. Runners flash past in peripheral vision. The
checkered finish banner appears ahead, growing rapidly with each rotation.

### SHOT 4 — SLOW-MOTION BREAK-THROUGH (3.0–4.0s)
Hard cut to **third-person, low angle, dramatic slow motion**. The three cubes
**smash through a checkered finish tape** stretched across the path. The tape
**tears in two** and both halves whip and ripple away in slow motion. Golden
confetti and sunlight burst outward. Silhouetted crowds line both sides of the
track with arms raised, cheering. A brief white impact flash on contact, then the
cubes sail through frame toward camera. **Hold the final beat** — cubes airborne,
tape fragments floating, confetti suspended. Speed ramps back to normal on the
last few frames.

**Ending:** final frame should be bright and open in the **center of the frame**
(app overlays a title there). End on the held triumphant beat, not a fade.
